GDPR / data protection design
Only store contact information needed for booking administration. Booking communications and marketing are kept separate. The database includes privacy-notice, lawful-basis and retention-review fields so a retention process can be added later. This supports good data-protection practice but does not by itself establish full UK GDPR compliance.
