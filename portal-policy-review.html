PM-01 · v0.1 · Draft

Portal terms, acceptable use and certificate integrity

Proposed reviewers: HR / Academy / Management
Review: annually and after significant legal, operational or system changes.

The portal is provided for authorised training, employment, compliance and operational purposes. Use your own account, keep credentials secure and report errors or suspected misuse promptly. Operatives may access their own records; authorised managers and supervisors may access records necessary for their assigned responsibilities. Academy staff may administer records within their approved authority.

Certificates and digital cards may be presented to authorised clients and auditors for legitimate verification, including by QR code. Do not alter certificates, impersonate another person, complete their assessment, create fraudulent evidence (including using AI), bypass access controls or disclose information for unauthorised purposes. Downloaded copies must be protected and disposed of securely when no longer required. Verify current status against the issuing organisation or authorised live record; a copy is not proof of current competence.

Suspected breaches may result in proportionate access restrictions and investigation. Deliberate fraud or serious misuse may constitute gross misconduct for employees, subject to the existing disciplinary policy, circumstances and fair procedure, including appeal. Contractor breaches are managed under applicable contractual arrangements. Nothing here restricts lawful reporting, whistleblowing or statutory rights (Acas, 2015; Great Britain, 1996; Great Britain, 1990).

PM-02 · v0.1 · Draft

Mobile app and personal device acceptable use

Proposed reviewers: HR / IT / Data protection lead
Review: annually and after significant legal, operational or system changes.

Use a supported device with screen locking, current security updates and a secure connection. Do not share credentials, leave confidential information accessible to others, or store certificates in unauthorised shared folders or cloud accounts. Report loss, theft or suspected compromise promptly through the company reporting route. Do not use the app while driving or when site conditions make device use unsafe.

Personal device use does not grant access to unrelated photos, messages, contacts or location. Any app permissions must be explained and necessary. Alternative arrangements should be available for mandatory training when personal devices are unsuitable. On leaving or losing authorisation, cease portal access and securely remove retained company information as directed, subject to lawful retention and legal holds (ICO, n.d.; Great Britain, 2018).

PM-03 · v0.1 · Draft

Privacy notice — review draft

Proposed reviewers: Data protection lead / Management
Review: annually and after significant legal, operational or system changes.

The proposed portal processes identity and contact information, employment or contractor details, role and reporting relationships, training and assessment records, certificates, account and security activity, and profile images where required. Purposes include training provision, competency verification, operational compliance, security and auditing.

Access is intended to be restricted according to role. Relevant certificate information may be shared with authorised clients and auditors through approved verification arrangements. External hosting or AI services may process limited information only under approved arrangements. Personal data must not be submitted to unapproved services.

RRT must confirm its controller identity, contact details, lawful basis for each purpose, recipient categories, international transfers and safeguards, retention periods, source of indirectly collected data, and whether any automated decision-making applies before this notice is issued. Individuals may exercise applicable rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and complain to the ICO. These rights are subject to statutory conditions. Acknowledging the notice is not consent to all processing (UK GDPR, arts 5, 6, 13–14; ICO, n.d.).

Not ready for issue: the controller and privacy contact, processing register, actual sharing arrangements and retention schedule must be verified and completed.

PM-04 · v0.1 · Draft

Training and assessment integrity — user rules

Proposed reviewers: Academy / HSEQ / IQA
Review: annually and after significant legal, operational or system changes.

Complete your own learning and assessments. Do not share answers to live assessments, impersonate another learner or submit false evidence. Report inaccurate training records and certificate discrepancies. Online completion evidences the specified learning outcomes only; it does not by itself authorise hazardous work or prove practical competence.

Where the job requires practical instruction, observed assessment or verified equivalent qualifications, complete those requirements before undertaking the activity. Follow current company safe systems of work and stop and seek guidance where risks cannot be controlled within your authority. RRT retains responsibility for adequate training and supervision (Great Britain, 1974, s.2; Great Britain, 1999, reg.13; HSE, n.d.).
