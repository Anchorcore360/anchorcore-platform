Access Level
Access Level controls what the person can see. It is separate from Job Title and Compliance Job Role. Office personnel can still use Operative access if they only need their own profile and learning.
Company & Reporting
Company and Manager are optional. People and companies remain separate records and can be linked later from the person's profile. If no manager is selected, the person is not linked to a reporting line.
Compliance
Compliance roles are optional. If left blank, the person still has a complete profile and QR code but no role-based compliance requirements. Job roles are managed from the Compliance section, including future office-specific roles.
