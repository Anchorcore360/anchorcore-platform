Workforce Sense Check

Periodically compare an authorised HR workforce list with RRTA. This is a checking tool only — it will not create, archive or edit people.

1 · Upload Current HR ListUse the latest workforce, starters/leavers or employee Excel file.
2 · Compare With RRTAThe file is compared with current and archived RRTA people using name, email and employee number where available.
3 · Review ExceptionsInvestigate flagged records and make any required change manually through Manage People.
On HR List · Not In RRTACheck whether a person needs adding.
In RRTA · Not On HR ListCheck whether they have left or the HR list scope differs.
Possible DuplicateReview matching names, emails or employee numbers.
Archived · Appears On HR ListPossible returning employee — consider reinstating their existing profile.
Excel workforce sense check

The comparison engine will read the spreadsheet for this check only. It will not use the spreadsheet to build or change employee records.

Data protection rule: no database changes are made from this page. A person missing from a spreadsheet is never automatically treated as a leaver. Returning employees should normally have their archived RRTA profile reinstated rather than creating another account.
