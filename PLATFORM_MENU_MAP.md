## Shared Data Rules

1. RRT Direct Staff remain RRT workforce records.
2. Subcontractors remain RRT workforce records but are classified separately.
3. External RRTA customers and delegates remain in the Academy customer/delegate data model.
4. A training booking can reference an RRT person or an external delegate without moving them into the other population.
5. RRTA achievements/accreditations for an RRT person feed back into their learner profile and workforce compliance.
6. Managers use one login for both My Learning and My Team.
7. RRTA administrators can access workforce administration through the Academy portal without duplicating workforce records.

## Core Workflow

Compliance Requirement → Training Need → Manager Training Request → RRTA Booking → Course Delivery → Attendance/Assessment → Achievement → Accreditation/Certificate → Compliance Recalculation
