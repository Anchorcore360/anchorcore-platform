SA006 – Safe Working in Civils

Generic course requirements. Your booking email/portal message contains your date, time and venue information.

About these joining instructions

Please read these course requirements before attending SA006. They apply each time this course is delivered unless the Training Academy issues a newer version.

PPE & Equipment Required

🥾
Safety Boots
⛑
Safety Helmet
🦺
Hi-Visibility Clothing
(Long Sleeve)
🧤
Safety Gloves
🥽
Eye Protection
🧥
Suitable clothing for inclement weather

PPE can be provided on request. Contact the Training Academy at least 48 hours before the course with the items and sizes you require.

Arrival & Attendance

Please arrive in good time for registration and follow the arrival time shown in your booking email or learner portal. Late arrivals will only be admitted at the trainer's discretion and in accordance with awarding-body requirements.

Clothing & Footwear

Clothing: Shorts are strictly prohibited during practical elements. Delegates must wear suitable long trousers.

Footwear: The classroom is a no-muddy-boots area. Please bring a change of shoes for classroom sessions and leave muddy boots safely in the practical area.

Additional Learning Support

If you have learning requirements or require reasonable adjustments to fully participate, please let the Training Academy know before your course. Information will be treated in confidence and shared only where necessary to support you effectively.

Before attending

  • Check your booking email or learner portal for the confirmed date, time and venue.
  • Read the location and parking instructions carefully.
  • Bring the required PPE and equipment or contact the Academy in advance if you need to borrow items.
  • Acknowledge the joining instructions when requested.
