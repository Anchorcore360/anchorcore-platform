Working Safely Around Fibre Installations

A short demonstration programme showing how a real online course can sit inside the RRTA learner portal, remember progress and report a final result back to the LMS.

1. Plan the task before starting

Before starting work, confirm the task, location, access arrangements and known hazards. A dynamic assessment should continue throughout the job, not just at the beginning.

Stop and reassess if the environment, customer situation, traffic conditions, access route or task changes.
  • Understand what work is authorised.
  • Check the work area and access route.
  • Confirm the controls and equipment required.
  • Escalate anything outside your competence or permission.

2. Work area, PPE and public protection

PPE supports safe working but does not replace planning or suitable controls. The work area should be organised so that colleagues, customers and members of the public are not exposed to unnecessary risk.

Good practice: keep the work area tidy, control tools and materials, maintain safe access and leave the location secure when work is complete.

Your exact PPE and site controls must follow the task-specific method, company rules and any client/network requirements.

3. Professional conduct in customer environments

Home-install and customer-facing work combines technical competence with professional behaviour. Explain what you are doing, protect the customer's property and ask permission before moving or altering anything.

  • Be respectful and clear.
  • Do not ignore hazards or unsafe conditions.
  • Protect the property and leave the work area tidy.
  • If a situation feels unsafe or inappropriate, stop and follow the escalation process.
